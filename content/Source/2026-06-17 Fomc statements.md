June 17, 2026

### Federal Reserve issues FOMC statement

For release at 2:00 p.m. EDT

- [Share](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260617a.htm# "Share")

The Federal Open Market Committee approved the following statement for release by a 12 – 0 vote:

The Committee decided to maintain the target range for the federal funds rate at 3-1/2 to 3-3/4 percent, in support of the Federal Reserve's dual mandate. The Committee reaffirmed its policy of maintaining <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">ample reserves in the banking system</mark>.

Economic activity is expanding at a solid pace despite elevated uncertainty that owes, in part, to the conflict in the Middle East. Productivity growth and capital investment are strong. <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">Job gains have kept pace with the workforce, and the unemployment rate has changed little.</mark>

<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">Inflation remains elevated relative to the Committee's 2 percent goal,</mark> in part reflecting supply shocks that have driven price increases in certain sectors, including energy. <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">The Committee will deliver price stability.</mark>
